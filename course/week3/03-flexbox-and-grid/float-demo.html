Before flexbox & grid: the float problem

Both boxes below use the exact same floated .card columns. The only difference: the second container has the clearfix class. Watch what the blue dashed box and the red bar do.

❌ Without a clearfix — the container collapses

The blue dashed box should wrap around the three cards. Instead it collapses to a thin strip at the top, and the red bar slides UP behind the cards — because floated elements leave normal flow, so their parent thinks it's empty (height = 0).
